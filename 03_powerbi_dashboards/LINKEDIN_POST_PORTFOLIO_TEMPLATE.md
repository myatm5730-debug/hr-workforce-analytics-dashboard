### 🔍 Key Business Insights Discovered:
1️⃣ **Turnover Hotspots:** Enterprise attrition stands at **18.8%** (94 resigned), with **HR (22.1%)** and **Engineering (19.3%)** experiencing the highest turnover rates.
2️⃣ **Payroll Disparity:** Engineering accounts for **17.5% of the workforce** but consumes **25.1% of the total 10.2B MMK gross payroll**.
3️⃣ **Overtime Burnout Risk:** Several operations roles recorded over **80+ OT hours/month**, where overtime pay constituted **>40% of their gross salary**—a direct indicator of headcount shortages and imminent burnout.
4️⃣ **Retention vs. Satisfaction:** High performers (Rating 4-5) with low satisfaction scores (<2) showed a 3x higher turnover probability within their first 18 months.
